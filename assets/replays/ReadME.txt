Your replay files will be saved at here.

Folder layout:
  base/                                  base-game songs (assets/ weeks)
  <mod>/                                 songs from that mod folder (folder name = mod folder)
  custom/                                custom charts (content/CustomCharts)
  content/VanillaSongs/<pack>/           content pack songs (Erect / PicoMix / BFMix / Collab ...)
  content/<group>/<pack>/                any other content group

Replays recorded by older builds may still sit in base/ - they are still listed.
